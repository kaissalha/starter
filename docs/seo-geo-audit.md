# SEO and GEO feature audit

Audited 2026-09-23 against OpenSEO `0ffff93`, DurableHub `7d780fc517`, and Starter `c8066944`. The Durable product was also inspected in the signed-in browser at `https://durable.com/b/nockta-upygmc0b/seo-geo` and its four detail pages. The inspected business has no connected listings or reviews and no completed AI rankings, so those populated states are assessed from source. No paid provider request, Google OAuth flow, or production crawl was run.

## Recommendation

Build one organization-scoped **Discoverability** workspace in Starter. Open with Durable's simple status and next-action view, then let users drill into OpenSEO-depth research and evidence. The existing Starter website is the default target; a connected primary domain becomes the canonical research target. Keep Google Search Console, Google Business Profile, and paid research optional, with explicit states for missing or stale data. Never present a setup score, estimated rank, or an AI model simulation as measured search visibility.

## Detailed findings

### OpenSEO's useful depth

- Its route and service boundaries already separate research, tracked state, technical audits, first-party Google data, reports and agent tools. This is the right **capability set**, not an architecture to copy into Starter's Next/oRPC/Workflow stack.
- Keyword research combines paid metrics and SERP inspection with saved, tagged and exportable work. Rank tracking makes market/device and spend explicit. These are much stronger than a generic “keyword opportunities” count.
- AI search has two different jobs: prompt exploration and measured brand/citation lookup. Its brand lookup caches paid calls and settles platforms independently; a partial provider failure is shown as such, not persisted as a successful zero. This failure handling should carry over.
- The site audit has a bounded crawl and explicit blocked/rate-limited/error classifications. Its current page reporters check missing or extreme titles/descriptions, H1 count/order, noindex/canonical signals, thin content, missing alt text, slow responses and link depth. It also has multipage checks. This provides a useful issue taxonomy and evidence contract.
- Google Search Console and GA4 are optional OAuth-backed integrations. Search Console supplies actual search clicks and impressions; provider keyword volume and modeled traffic are estimates. The product keeps those distinct.
- OpenSEO has a large number of controls and project routes. Showing all of them at the top level would overwhelm Starter's small-business audience. Default filters and saved views should be progressive, with actions phrased around pages and customers.

### DurableHub's useful simplicity and limits

- The overview is immediately understandable: four areas (listings, keywords, reviews, AI), an action list with effort, and one detail page per area. Its keyword strategy turns research into a service-page or blog action, which maps well to Starter.
- Its score is a weighted setup/completeness index, not a direct measure of search visibility: publishing earns 10 points, GBP contributes up to 35, directories up to 20, and AI ranks up to 15 (`discoverability-score.ts`). The visible 0–100 gauge calls this “Visibility”. Starter should label any similar composite **readiness** and keep actual clicks, positions and mentions beside it.
- The automatic-checks UI marks LLM readability, mobile, H1, alt text and titles as passed by default, while indexing is conditional (`auto-handled-checks.tsx`). Starter should compute these from the published document or a crawl, and use “not checked” when evidence is absent. An `llms.txt` file by itself is not evidence that AI providers read or cite a site.
- Directory research and AI model probes are expensive/background work with processing states, polling and refresh limits. Starter needs durable jobs, bounded concurrency, cache/refresh policy, provider provenance and error state before presenting them as dependable features.
- The live example has little populated data and some disabled actions. It verifies navigation and language, not the quality of rankings, listings or review sync.

### Starter's current foundation and gaps

- `@starter/infinite-website` already stores localized page SEO title/description/image; the public renderer emits title, description, Open Graph, canonical, locale alternates and `x-default`. The public sitemap includes website and Blog pages. These should be extended rather than replaced.
- `apps/websites/src/lib/website-metadata.ts` indexes a site only with `WEBSITE_CRAWLER_POLICY=public` **and** a connected primary domain. Public-page metadata otherwise uses `noindex`. The SEO workspace must expose this actual gate as the first fixable condition. Customer sites now serve `/robots.txt` (a Sitemap line only for indexable hosts), a generated icon and apple-icon, and an `/llms.txt` for indexable hosts; `llms.txt` is a deterministic index and is not evidence of AI citation.
- Starter's Tinybird analytics already records AI referrers, visits, conversions and web vitals. Those are useful impact signals but cannot substitute for Search Console search performance or AI citations.
- There is no current DataForSEO service/dependency, GSC/GA4/GBP OAuth connection, SEO persistence model, rank scheduler or crawl workflow in Starter. A memory note referred to a DataForSEO module, but the checked tree at `c8066944` does not contain it.
- The existing `oauth_connections` provider enum contains only Gmail and Google Calendar. Google SEO scopes need an intentionally designed connection/ownership model; a sign-in account is not automatically authorized to read Search Console or GBP data.

## Product shape

1. **Overview:** Website search status, real search clicks when connected, sampled AI mentions when available, top three actions, and a compact readiness checklist. Unknown and stale states are explicit. “Get discovered” is the headline; “SEO/GEO” can remain the navigation label.
2. **Content opportunities:** Suggested keywords with demand/intent/competition, own-site coverage, competitor overlap and a clear “create page”, “write post”, or “improve page” action. A research drawer exposes SERP details, filters and export.
3. **Search performance:** Search Console queries/pages and selected rank history, split from website visits/conversions. Market/device selectors live here, not on the overview.
4. **Site health:** Published-site audit with issue evidence, affected URLs, severity, fix destination and rerun history. Show publish/indexing problems before minor title-length guidance.
5. **AI answers:** Fixed customer questions, platform, date, mention/citation evidence, competitor references and source URLs. Manual exploration is a separate advanced action. Never imply AI results are exhaustive or stable.
6. **Presence:** Local listings, GBP reviews and requests for eligible local businesses; backlinks and referring domains under an advanced tab. Do not equate a directory listing with a backlink or a GBP review with an organic ranking.
7. **Reports and agent:** Reuse the same services through oRPC and MCP. Reports snapshot data and source dates; agent writes require existing approval rules.

For every provider call: validate organization ownership and target scope; cap request size, frequency and concurrency; record cost/source/status/timestamp; cache identical research; preserve partial failure; and never persist untrusted provider HTML as executable content. Crawls must enforce the existing URL/host ownership policy, DNS/IP checks, robots behavior, page/bytes/time caps and clear blocked-site reporting. Site data must be stored as typed relational snapshots, with organization and website foreign keys. A background job should be idempotent and retain earlier successful results if a refresh fails.

## Acceptance conditions

- A newly generated, unpublished site shows actionable publish/indexing status without invented traffic, rankings or citations.
- A published site on a connected primary domain can be audited; each issue has an affected URL, observed value, severity and fix target. A blocked/partial crawl cannot claim a healthy site.
- Keyword research and rank checks use an explicit market/device and display provider date and estimated spend before a metered action. Repeated requests use cache or a documented refresh rule.
- Search Console clicks/impressions/CTR/position and Tinybird visits/conversions have separate labels, date ranges and empty/error states.
- AI visibility retains the prompt, exact answer/citation evidence, platform, timestamp and competitor comparison. A failed platform is not counted as “not mentioned”.
- Local listings distinguish discovered, claimed, verified, unlisted and unknown; review replies are approval-gated.
- Every read and mutation is organization-scoped, all provider payloads are validated, and agent/MCP output contains source and freshness. English and Arabic UI, mobile and RTL flows work.
- Focused tests, `bun check`, browser flows, provider integration checks and a published-site crawl provide evidence before calling the whole feature complete.

## Current implementation status

The first Starter slice adds an organization-scoped SEO/GEO dashboard route and a published-website overview. It checks the selected primary domain and localized page titles and descriptions, and links to the Website editor and existing traffic analytics. Indexing is explicitly marked **not checked** because the dashboard cannot verify the public renderer's crawler policy or search-engine index state.

The GEO panel now generates three customer questions from the organization website brief, scopes every question to its saved business location, and compares sampled answers from OpenAI, Gemini, and Claude. It also accepts a custom question, applies the same location boundary server-side, and checks whether the website's business name appears. Questions are cached for seven days; answers are cached for 24 hours and limited to 12 uncached questions per hour. These are sampled model answers without live web search or citations. They do not measure ChatGPT product placement, AI Overview appearances, or search share of voice. OpenSEO's Prompt Explorer also supports Perplexity, web search by country, citations, related queries, shareable URL state, and local search history; its separate Brand Lookup supplies measured ChatGPT/Google AI Overview mentions, cited pages and questions, a 12-month trend, and competitor share of voice. Starter does not yet provide those provider-backed measurements, a rendered-site crawl, keyword research, Search Console data, organic rank tracking, backlinks, listings, reviews or reports. Those capabilities remain the stages above, not completed features.

## External API realities

DataForSEO's [keyword volume](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/keywords_data-google_ads-search_volume-live/), [live organic SERP](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/serp-se-type-live-advanced/) and [backlinks summary](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/backlinks-summary-live/) endpoints are metered. Google's [Search Analytics API](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/searchanalytics/query) returns top rows rather than a guaranteed complete query set. Google Business Profile [review APIs](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/review-data) require a separately authorized application and OAuth access. These constraints shape the UI and rollout; they are not provider credentials or live integration verification.
